On October 17, 1996, a small, three person agency filed for a business license in San Francisco City Hall. Dozens of digital careers started there, among teammates, clients, partners, and friends who were figuring out interactive marketing together.

Many lifelong business associations, and even more friendships were started there. This page is for them: to share a memory, reconnect, and see where everyone landed.
