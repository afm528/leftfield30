# Left Field is 30

The page for Left Field's 30th anniversary: October 17, 1996 to October 17, 2026.

**Live site:** https://leftfield30.netlify.app

It's one static file, `index.html`: HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, with no build step. Improvements from anyone who worked at, with, or alongside Left Field are welcome.

## Suggest a change

**In your browser (easiest):**
1. Open [`index.html`](index.html) and click the pencil icon, or use the **Edit this page on GitHub** link in the site's footer.
2. Make your change and click **Commit changes…**. GitHub will fork the repo for you and open a pull request.
3. Netlify builds a preview of your change and posts the link on the pull request.
4. Once it's approved and merged, the live site updates automatically in about a minute.

**Locally:** fork, clone, open `index.html` in a browser, edit, and open a pull request against `main`.

## What's where in `index.html`

- **Brand palette and fonts:** the `:root` block at the top of `<style>`. Lime, brick, bright green and black come from the 1998 and 1999 leftfield.net homepages and the original t-shirt.
- **Logo:** the `<symbol id="lf-mark">` SVG near the top of `<body>`. It's redrawn from the t-shirt, so if you have the original vector artwork, please send it in.
- **Reunion details, links and email:** the `CONFIG` block and the `START`/`END` times in the `<script>` at the bottom.
- **Tributes:** LinkedIn embeds get pasted into the `#wall` section, newest first.

## Ground rules

- Keep the brand: the palette, the reversed F in LEFTFIELD, and "Marketing for a Wired World™".
- Keep it one self-contained file, with no frameworks or build tools.
- Check it on a phone-width screen before submitting.
- Facts about Left Field's history should be ones you can vouch for.

Want to share a memory instead of editing code? Post on LinkedIn with **#LeftField30**, or use the email link on the site.
